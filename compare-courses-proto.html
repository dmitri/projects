Guitar Chords 101

12-Week Course • $1,575

Description

Learn Berklee's approach to chord construction, focusing on the notes that chords are based around rather than just shapes. Master triads, 7th chords, inversions, and voicings to unlock the fretboard and gain freedom on your instrument.

Learning Outcomes

  • Play chord voicings from lead sheets in various musical styles
  • Construct triads, seventh chords, extended voicings, and inversions
  • Master major, minor, diminished, augmented, and seventh chord voicings
  • Play triads over bass notes and alterations

Syllabus Preview

12 Lessons including:
• Triads & Inversions
• Barre Chords & Power Chords
• Seventh Chords: Drop 2s & Drop 3s
• Guide Tone Chords
• Triads over Bass Notes

Acoustic Guitar Techniques

12-Week Course • $1,575

Description

Become a more effective acoustic guitarist through strengthened rhythmic strumming, alternate tunings, and fingerstyle playing. Features in-depth techniques for improving fluency, tone, dynamics, and control.

Learning Outcomes

  • Play and compose in various non-standard tunings
  • Use practice techniques to refine fingerstyle and strumming
  • Achieve increased control, evenness, and fluency
  • Understand phrasing in multi-voiced fingerstyle pieces
  • Connect technique and position with instrument tone
  • Integrate melodic lines into accompaniments

Syllabus Preview

12 Lessons including:
• Open Triad Tunings
• Travis Picking Techniques
• Modal Approaches in Open Tunings
• Fingerstyle Fundamentals
• Advanced Strumming & Triplets
